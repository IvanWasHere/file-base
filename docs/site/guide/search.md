## Starting a search

Press <kbd>⌘F</kbd> or click the 🔍 button in the toolbar. A search bar opens at the top of the active pane. Start typing and the results replace the listing.

Close the search with the **×** at the right of the bar, or <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to get your listing back.

## This Folder or Subfolders

Pick where to search with the buttons to the right of the search box:

- **This Folder** filters what's already on screen. It's instant.
- **Subfolders** searches the current folder **and everything inside it**. Results show up as they're found, and the bar tells you how many matches there are.

Results are ordinary items. You can open, preview, copy, drag or delete them straight from the result list.

## Filters

![The filter row: kind, size, modified date, type and hidden files]({{ '/assets/img/guide/search-filters.png' | relative_url }})

Click the **filters** button (the sliders icon) to narrow things down further:

| Filter | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Show** | All items, Files only, Folders only |
| **Size** | Under 100 KB, 100 KB – 10 MB, 10 MB – 100 MB, 100 MB – 1 GB, Over 1 GB |
| **Modified** | Past 24 hours, past week, past month, past year |
| **Type** | File extensions, e.g. `png, jpg` |
| **Hidden files** | Include hidden files in the search |

Filters work in both This Folder and Subfolders, and with an empty search box they filter by themselves. For example: Type `pdf`, Size 10 MB – 100 MB, Subfolders — every mid-sized PDF under the current folder.
