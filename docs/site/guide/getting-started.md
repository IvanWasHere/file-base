From top to bottom and left to right:

## Menu bar and tabs

The **File, Edit, View and Go** menus hold every command in the app. The same menus appear in the macOS menu bar at the top of the screen. Keyboard shortcuts are listed next to each command.

Under the menus is the **tab bar**. Each tab is a separate workspace with its own folder, or several folders if you split it. Click **+** on the right to open a new tab.

## Toolbar

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ← → | Back and forward through the folders you've visited in this pane |
| ↑ | Go to the enclosing folder |
| ⟳ | Refresh the listing (it normally updates on its own) |
| New Folder | Create a folder here and start naming it |
| 🔍 Search | Open the search bar for this pane |
| # | Calculate checksums for the selected files |
| Path | Where you are. Click any part of it to jump there |
| ☆ Star | Add this folder to Favorites, or remove it |
| Layout picker | Choose how the tab is split into panes |
| View picker | Details, icons or Photos |
| 👁 Eye | Show or hide the preview panel |

## Sidebar

The **narrow rail** on the far left is always visible. It has one-click buttons for Home, Desktop, Documents, Applications, Downloads and Trash, plus a button that shows or hides the wide sidebar.

The **wide sidebar** lists:

- **Quick Access**: Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Applications, Pictures, Music, Movies and Trash.
- **Favorites**: folders you've starred. Use the ☆ button in the toolbar, or right-click a folder and choose **Add to Favorites**.
- **Recent**: folders you've opened lately.
- **Drives**: your startup disk and anything mounted, such as external drives, with their free space.
- **Storage**: how full each drive is.

Hide the wide sidebar with <kbd>⌥⌘S</kbd> when you want more room.

## The listing

The middle of the window shows the folder's contents. Click to select, double-click to open. [Views](../views/) covers the different ways to display a folder.

## Preview panel

The panel on the right shows details about the selected item: a picture preview, the first part of a text file, size, dates and permissions, and camera data for photos. It doesn't open by itself. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> or click the 👁 button to show or hide it. More in [Preview and photos](../preview/).

## Status bar

Along the bottom: how many items are in the folder, how many are selected and their total size. On the right is the current split layout and view.

## Everything comes back

When you quit and reopen FileBase, you get back exactly what you had: your tabs, their split layouts, every pane's folder and history, your favorites and your view choices.
