## Showing the panel

The preview panel stays closed until you ask for it, so selecting files never squeezes your listing. To show or hide it:

- press <kbd>Space</kbd> (like Quick Look in Finder),
- click the 👁 button at the right end of the toolbar, or
- choose **View → Show Preview**.

Close it with the **×** in its corner, or with any of the above. It stays how you left it, even after a restart.

## What it shows

For whatever is selected, the panel shows:

- **A preview**: the picture for images, the opening lines for text and code files, the document for PDFs, and a large icon for everything else.
- **Details**: type, size, when it was modified and created, and its permissions.
- **The full path**, at the bottom.

Large text files are cut off after the first part, and the panel says so. To read the whole thing, use the [text reader](../text-reader/).

## Photo details

![The preview panel showing dimensions, colour profile and camera data for a photo]({{ '/assets/img/guide/image-metadata.png' | relative_url }})

For images, the panel adds the information an image editor would show: **dimensions, megapixels, aspect ratio, resolution, colour mode and profile, and orientation**. For photos from a camera or phone, it adds the **camera model, lens and exposure settings**, and for geotagged photos, **where it was taken**.

## Photos view

To look through a whole folder of pictures, switch it to **Photos view** with <kbd>⌘5</kbd>. See [Views](../views/#photos).
